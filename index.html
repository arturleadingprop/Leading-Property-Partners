What is SSH?

Housing for
Vulnerable Adults

Specialist Supported Housing provides accommodation and care support to vulnerable adults including those with learning disabilities, mental health conditions, physical disabilities, or those transitioning out of care.

The UK government has a statutory duty to house these individuals and funds their accommodation through Housing Benefit, a government-backed income stream paid directly to property investors via regulated care providers.

This creates one of the most stable, secure, and ethically positive investment propositions available in the UK property market today.

Finance & Taxes

A practical introduction to the main financial and tax considerations investors should understand when buying UK property.

Investor Guide

Understand the costs before you invest.

Property investment can involve acquisition costs, finance costs, ongoing tax considerations and potential taxes when a property is sold. The treatment depends on your circumstances, the property and how the investment is structured.

Stamp Duty Land Tax

Stamp Duty Land Tax (SDLT) can apply when purchasing residential property in England and Northern Ireland. The amount depends on factors including the purchase price, the property type, whether you already own property and your residency and company status.

Because SDLT rules and rates can change, investors should confirm the current position for their transaction before exchanging contracts.

Rental Income & Income Tax

Rental income can have tax consequences for individual investors. The taxable position can depend on the ownership structure, allowable expenses and your wider income and circumstances.

Keeping accurate records of rental income and eligible costs is important throughout ownership.

Limited Company Ownership

Some investors choose to hold property through a limited company. This can change how rental profits, finance costs and withdrawals are taxed and can introduce additional company reporting and administration.

Company structures are not automatically better or worse; they should be assessed against your objectives with a qualified accountant or tax adviser.

Capital Gains Tax

Capital Gains Tax (CGT) may arise when an investment property is sold for a gain. The calculation and applicable reliefs can depend on the ownership structure, the investor's circumstances and the nature of the property.

Always obtain current professional advice before planning a disposal.

Mortgage & Finance Costs

Financing can affect both the cash required to purchase a property and the ongoing investment return. Lenders assess affordability, rental coverage, loan-to-value and other criteria.

Mortgage interest and other finance costs can have different tax treatment depending on how the property is owned, so the finance decision should be considered alongside the tax position.

Buying From Outside the UK

Non-UK residents can invest in UK property, but additional considerations may apply around finance, tax, reporting and currency. The rules depend on your residence, domicile, ownership structure and the property itself.

We recommend taking advice from UK professionals who regularly work with international property investors.

Important: Leading Property Partners is not a tax or financial adviser. This page is for general educational purposes only and does not constitute tax, legal, mortgage or investment advice. Rules can change and individual circumstances differ. Obtain independent professional advice before making a decision.
